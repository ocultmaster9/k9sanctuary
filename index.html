A safe place, built by hand and heart

K9 Sanctuary was founded by Lenuța Nedelcu, who has poured her own life and savings into giving abandoned dogs a second chance in a region where too many are left behind. Vrancea County has struggled with shelter neglect and cruelty — we exist to be the opposite.

Every day the dogs get fresh food and clean water, and are let out to run — with someone always there to make sure they’re safe, and to give them the love they were denied.

Romanian taxpayer? Redirect 3.5% — it’s free.

You can send 3.5% of income tax you already owe to the shelter at no cost to you, through Form 230. It doesn’t cost you a cent — it just tells the state to send part of your tax to the dogs. Deadline for 2025 income: 25 May 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Where does my donation go?

Straight to the dogs — food, veterinary care, and rebuilding the shelter with warmer kennels and stronger fencing. We publish how funds are used.

Can I donate from outside Romania?

Absolutely. We accept international donations by card and PayPal, by Revolut, and by bank transfer, in euros. Dogs are cared for and adopted across Europe.

Is this a registered non-profit?

Yes — Asociația A Chance for Dogs Go, a registered Romanian non-profit association listed in the ANAF register of non-profit entities.

How else can I help?

Share our dogs, follow us on TikTok, or talk to us about adoption or fostering. Awareness saves lives too.
